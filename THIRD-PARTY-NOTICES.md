crates take no external dependencies at all, and the crates that do pull theirs
from crates.io under the usual permissive terms.

anything derived from them, and `crates/verb-synonyms/src/synonym_groups.tsv` is

## 12dicts 6.0.2 and AGID — Alan Beale, Kevin Atkinson

The frequency ranking and the lemmatisation used to build the same table come
from `Lemmatized/2+2+3frq.txt` in Alan Beale's 12dicts package, version 6.0.2.
Beale releases the 12dicts lists into the public domain but notes that the
`2+2+3` lists depend on Kevin Atkinson's Automatically Generated Inflection
Database (AGID) and are therefore governed by AGID's terms, which he does not
add to:

> The 12dicts lists were compiled by Alan Beale. I explicitly release them to
> the public domain, but request acknowledgment of their use. (Actually, the
> dependency of the 2of12inf list and the 2+2+3 lists on AGID prevents their
> release into the public domain. However, I do not impose any additional
> requirements on their use beyond those imposed by AGID and its sources, as
> described in agid.txt.)

AGID's terms, from `agid.txt`:

> Copyright 2000 by Kevin Atkinson
>
> Permission to use, copy, modify, distribute and sell this database, the

> documentation for any purpose is hereby granted without fee, provided that

> copyright notice and this permission notice appear in supporting
> documentation. Kevin Atkinson makes no representations about the suitability

AGID is in turn built from the Moby part-of-speech database and the Moby word

one above with "1.6 Copyright 1997" in place of "3.0 Copyright 2006".

Beale asks for acknowledgment rather than requiring it; this file is that
